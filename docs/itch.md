## Description

It is 1:40 in the morning. The Last Call closed at midnight, every light in the place is on, and Danny Roarke is dead on the floor of his own bar. The police are two hours out. Four people stayed. One of them is you.

Frank Sable is a detective who is useless sober and brilliant drunk. **SHIFT** between the two:

- **Sober Frank** sees what is in front of him, and people talk to him.
- **The Voice** — the sharp, cruel thing that takes over when he drinks — sees what they are hiding. Nobody confesses to the Voice.

So you drink to look, and sober up to be heard. The same room holds different things for each of you: look closer at the body, the rail, the office, the jukebox, and find out which Frank can see what.

- Fully voiced, about 27 minutes for a careful first run
- Four suspects, each with something they cannot explain — show them the right thing, as the right Frank
- A clock that runs out at 4:00 AM, three drinks, and decisions that will not wait for you
- Lay the whole night out in order to reach the truth; seven endings

**Controls:** Space / click to advance · 1–9 choose · Shift to drink or sober up · E evidence board · Esc pause. Click the room to look at things.

The browser build adjusts its own quality to hold a steady frame rate. The desktop builds are the best way to see it.
